# KIN YOUTUBE CHANNEL PRODUCTION TEAM

## The channel
- Name: KIN
- Owner: KIN Creatives, a video editing and motion design agency in the Philippines. Founder and final approver: Justine.
- Format: faceless, narrated long form videos, about 20 minutes, in English for a global audience.
- Content pillars:
  1. Mysteries: unsolved cases, strange events, internet mysteries.
  2. Rise and fall: people, creators, and brands that blew up and then collapsed.
  3. Business stories: how companies were built, broke, or changed an industry.
- Main style reference: SunnyV2. Story first, cold open hook, strong narration, chapter structure, internet clips as evidence, fair but honest commentary.
- Visuals: motion graphics and 3D made in-house (After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve), plus B-roll and clips from the internet used as commentary. NO AI-generated visuals of any kind.
- Narration: a hired voice actor reads the final script.
- Shorts: 3 to 5 per long video.
- Goals: (1) ad revenue, (2) showcase KIN Creatives' editing and motion design, (3) build the KIN brand.
- Upload cadence: [TBD by Justine]

## Team
- Humans: Justine (final approver), KIN editors and motion designers, the hired voice actor.
- AI agents: content-strategist, scriptwriter, fact-checker, copywriter, social-media-manager.

## Pipeline
content-strategist (brief) > scriptwriter (dossier, script, VO script) > fact-checker (report, run in a fresh session) > scriptwriter (fixes) > voice actor and editors > copywriter (title, description, Shorts captions) > social-media-manager (calendar, clips, weekly report) > back to content-strategist.

## House rules (every agent, every output)
- Never use em dashes. Use commas, colons or periods.
- Be specific: numbers, timestamps, examples. No vague advice.
- If key information is missing, ask up to 3 short questions before working.
- Label every assumption with [ASSUMPTION].
- Label anything that needs checking with [VERIFY]. Never invent quotes, dates, numbers, sources or statistics.
- Real people are treated fairly. Allegations are attributed, never stated as fact.
- Keep everything advertiser friendly: no gratuitous violence, shock, or misleading clickbait.
- Platform features and policies change often. Say when a claim may be out of date.
- Nothing is published, posted, scheduled or sent without Justine's approval.

## Project folders
- bible/: series-bible.md, voice-guide.md, source-standards.md, legal-checklist.md. Every agent reads series-bible.md and voice-guide.md before creative work.
- strategy/: topic lists, briefs, calendars, analytics exports.
- episodes/<slug>/: brief.md, dossier.md, script.md, vo-script.md, factcheck.md, copy.md, social.md.
- social/: posting calendars, platform-specs.md, weekly reports.
- brand/: design-kit.md (fonts, colors, recurring graphics, templates). Read it before planning thumbnails, on-screen text, or motion graphics.
- feedback/: one log per agent. Read your own log before every task and follow every rule in it.
- drafts/: staging and old agent versions. Not live. See drafts/README.md.

## Handoff rule
Every agent saves its output as a file in the right folder and ends with a short HANDOFF block: what was done, what is still open, and who works on it next.
