# SOURCE STANDARDS

Every factual claim in a KIN video needs a source from tier 1 to 3. Tiers 4 and 5 help you find leads but cannot carry a claim alone, except history, which may rest on tier 4 books and academic work.

KIN covers stories from anywhere in the world. Use the official bodies of the country where the events happened.

## Source tiers (strongest first)
| Tier | Source type | Examples | How to use |
| 1 | Court decisions and official records | Court filings and judgments, regulatory filings (for example SEC records), company annual reports, government reports and statistics, legislative records | Strongest. Quote case numbers, filing names, and dates. |
| 2 | Official statements | Police, prosecutors, regulators, the company or person involved (press releases, official statements, briefings) | One side's account. Say who said it. |
| 3 | Established news with named reporters | National outlets, wire services, reputable regional and trade publications | Prefer 2 or more outlets that agree. Note the date: early reports are often wrong. |
| 4 | Books and academic work | Published histories, biographies, business books, theses, journal articles | Strong for history and background. Check the author's credentials. |
| 5 | Leads only | Wikipedia, forums, Reddit, social media posts, TikTok, other YouTube videos, blogs | Never a source. Follow the link to the original. Exception: a person's own post can prove that they said it, not that it is true. |

## Rules
- Open every page you cite. A search snippet is not a source.
- Record for every source: title, outlet or author, date published, link, date accessed, tier.
- Contested claims need sources from both sides, and the script must show both.
- Numbers (deaths, dates, amounts, valuations, revenue) need tier 1 to 3. If sources disagree, say so and give the range.
- Money: give the original currency and US dollars, and say which year's value it is.
- Quotes need the exact wording and the original speaker. Otherwise paraphrase.
- Rumor, legend, and internet theories are labeled as belief ("people online claimed", "the story goes"), never stated as fact.
- If a claim cannot be sourced, it is cut or marked [VERIFY] until it is.

## Citation format
[S#] Title. Outlet or author. Date published. Link. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Tier #.
